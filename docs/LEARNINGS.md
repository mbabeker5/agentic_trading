# Learnings register

Confirmed learnings only, one line each, dated, newest first. A learning is confirmed when it has been observed at least once in the live paper run or proven by a test, and it has changed something (a rule, a check, a doc). Proposals live in the journal until then.

Format: `YYYY-MM-DD | area | what we learned | what changed (commit)`

- 2026-09-08 | watchdog | A loss of internet on the Mac looks to the watchdog exactly like a dead Gateway. From about 12:33 to 12:55 the Mac had no working network (its own Slack alerts failed on a DNS lookup at the same minutes), the real read check timed out, the watchdog restarted a Gateway that was fine, and the new one sat on "connection error, will retry" for 19 minutes until the network came back. Four ticks were missed and the dead man's handle fired. | Backlog item 21: check the internet is reachable before blaming the Gateway, and say "no internet" when it is not. Written up in journal/2026-09-08.md
- 2026-09-08 | safety | The dead man's handle fired for real during market hours for the first time, at 13:00:06 on a 23.7 minute old heartbeat. It read the account, found nothing held and no orders working, and did not trade. | Nothing to change; the safe path is now seen working once
- 2026-09-08 | loop | The 240 second tick cap and the ten minute pre-flight budget both fired for real on their first live morning. The 09:30 tick hung and was killed at 09:34:43, and the 09:35 tick ran on time; the pre-flight wrote its verdict at 09:00:05. Yesterday the same two hangs cost 1 h 48 min and 44 min. | Nothing to change (8e2a509, b9c6a62 proven)
- 2026-09-08 | preflight | The pre-flight compared positions on its own and never read the orphan forgiveness file, so the one forgiven SPY share failed the whole day at 09:00. | Pre-flight now calls the loop's reconciliation with the shared reader in agent/orphans.py; forgiven orphans and untagged orders are notes, not failures (2c66f0e)
- 2026-09-08 | data | With the Gateway freshly logged in at 12:55 and no competing session error in sight, IBKR answered SPY quote requests with 10089 and 10168, "not subscribed to real time data for the API, delayed available / not enabled", on every watchdog run from 13:00 to 15:55. Whatever the 4.50 USD purchase on 2026-09-06 bought, the paper API is not seeing a real time entitlement. | Proposal to Mo in journal/2026-09-08.md: check the subscription covers API access and is shared with paper. Supersedes the 2026-09-02 line below that said sharing was enabled
- 2026-09-08 | reconcile | The "not checked" wording from backlog item 19 was seen live at 12:35: the tick could not read the broker, said so, marked the orphan check as not run, and halted nobody. | Nothing to change (7801267 proven)
- 2026-09-07 | gateway | A logged in Gateway can be useless. Twice today (overnight and at 15:52) the process was up, port 4002 open and the login handshake fine while every read hung, IBKR warning 2110. The three old watchdog checks passed throughout. | Watchdog `ib_answers` does a bounded real positions read after connecting and restarts on a miss; the 15:52 loss was caught and repaired by it unattended, restart proven four ways in 65 s (122254d to ba1a769)
- 2026-09-07 | schedule | Only the dead man's handle and the day trade rule read the holiday list, so on Labor Day book D swept and picked and books A, B and E waited for a 09:30 open that never came. | Every book reads `schedule.holidays` and shows phase closed for the whole day, seen live from the 10:58 tick (f8e719a)
- 2026-09-07 | mcp | A socket read timeout does not bound a call whose server keeps the reply open: a portfolio read ran 1,240 s with a 60 s timeout set, and the tick it belonged to hung 1 h 48 min. | Wall clock deadline on every MCP read (2a90b2b), 240 s cap on one tick (8e2a509), 10 minute budget on the pre-flight (b9c6a62)
- 2026-09-07 | operations | The trading MacBook slept on battery from about 07:50 to 09:43 New York and every failure of the morning traces to it: Gateway lost upstream, the tick hung, the pre-open wake ups and the pre-flight did not run on time. `caffeinate` cannot stop a lid closed sleep. | Mo alerted by Slack and screen at 09:50; standing instruction in journal/hub_status.md to plug in with the lid open before 07:00 on trading days
- 2026-09-06 | data | IBKR scanner filters are not all-or-nothing. Hours after Mo paid for market data, priceAbove and volumeAbove returned 50 rows on the same account where stVolume5MinAbove and marketCapAbove still returned zero rows with 162 then 365. A half-enabled filter set is worse than a disabled one, because a result gives no clue which kind you got. | Scanner sends no filters at all and does every check in our own code; pre-flight probes two filters, not one, and records both (this commit)
- 2026-09-06 | scanner | HIGH_STVOLUME_5MIN exists on this Gateway (527 scan codes listed by reqScannerParameters) and returns 50 rows unfiltered, as do TOP_PERC_GAIN, TOP_PERC_LOSE, HOT_BY_VOLUME and the MOST_ACTIVE control. Four scans plus a control is five requests, so the enrichment cap dropped from 40 to 35 to stay inside the 60 per ten minutes. | Momentum scanner runs four unfiltered scans, tags long and short, and exits 3 without writing on a ScanFailure (this commit)
- 2026-09-06 | gateway | Error 165 "Trading TWS session is connected from a different IP address" makes every scan, filtered or not, return zero rows, and it clears on its own. Same family as 10197: one market data session per account. | Already a hard error in the truth check, so the run exits 3 rather than reporting an empty market (this commit)
- 2026-09-06 | gateway | stop_gateway.sh silently did nothing: IBC's commandsend.sh needs telnet, which macOS no longer ships, so the hub's Gateway restart after Mo bought market data never stopped the old process. | stop script rewritten with a Python socket to the IBC command port and a kill-by-process fallback; IBC command port enabled on the next start (this commit)
- 2026-09-06 | watchdog | A restart script exiting 0 proves nothing. The watchdog must confirm a restart actually happened: new process id and a fresh login time in the IBC log. | Watchdog verifies all three (new pid, newer login, port open) within 120 seconds or alerts "restart did not take" (c0d29f9)

- 2026-09-06 | data | On this account, IBKR scanner filters fail silently: zero rows plus error 162 "Scanner filter X is disabled" in a callback nobody reads. A control scan with no filters returned 50 rows. | Scanner truth check raises on any 162, 165, 365, short control or timeout (eb74cdb)
