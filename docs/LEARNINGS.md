# Learnings register

Confirmed learnings only, one line each, dated, newest first. A learning is confirmed when it has been observed at least once in the live paper run or proven by a test, and it has changed something (a rule, a check, a doc). Proposals live in the journal until then.

Format: `YYYY-MM-DD | area | what we learned | what changed (commit)`

- 2026-09-10 | preflight | A pre-flight that cannot reach Gateway can end without writing anything at all, and a missing verdict was indistinguishable from a passing one. Today's 09:00 run left neither `output/preflight_2026-09-10.json` nor `output/NO_TRADE_TODAY`, so when Gateway came back at 11:27 nothing told the loop to stay out. Nothing in `agent/loop.py` had ever read a verdict file, so the contract "no pre-flight pass, no trade" was written down in three places and enforced in none. Only dry run mode kept the day safe, and mode is not the contract. | The pre-flight now guards every check and writes its verdict from a `finally` block, so a run that throws or is killed still leaves a fail on disk with `NO_TRADE_TODAY` and an alert (`dc8d22d`). The loop refuses entries, exits untouched, when today's verdict is missing, unreadable or not `pass`, and says so once per book per day (`6f67f0d`)
- 2026-09-10 | watchdog | A patience with no clock on it is not patience, it is a rule that never fires. The refusal to restart a Gateway whose process is alive but whose port is shut is right for the first few minutes and wrong after that, and it cost the mornings of both 2026-09-09 and 2026-09-10, over eight hours each. Stopping first is what removes the risk the refusal was guarding against, because `agent/stop_gateway.sh` kills the process, so there can never be two Gateways. | After 15 minutes of a shut port on a live process, with the market open or opening inside 90 minutes, the watchdog now stops Gateway and starts it again, once per outage, still silent between 01:45 and 02:30 and still never on error 10197. An unproved attempt on this path is recorded anyway, or a rule that fires every wake up would restart Gateway every five minutes. A verified restart now says so too (`95aede6`)
- 2026-09-09 | watchdog | The watchdog will not restart a Gateway whose process is up but whose port is shut, and there is no time limit on that patience. `_may_restart` only allows a port failure to trigger a restart when no Gateway process is running at all, on the reasoning that a shut port on a live process may just be a slow start. Today that process was wedged from 03:01 to 12:20 and the watchdog alerted eleven times without one repair attempt, 8 h 20 min of the trading day. The one check allowed to stop and restart a wedged Gateway, `ib_answers`, has to connect through the port to notice anything, so it was blind to it. | Backlog item 23: allow a stop-then-start once the port has been shut for a bounded time. Written up in journal/2026-09-09.md

- 2026-09-09 | data | Delayed prices are usable and delayed volume is not. 810 snapshots between 12:25 and 16:05 all carried a price, with SPY bid and ask a cent apart, the first day this account has had prices at all. The five minute historical bars carry believable volume in the same file: SPY's 15:45 bar shows 572,657 shares. | Backlog item 25: prefer the bar volume, drop the ceiling to about 10 billion
