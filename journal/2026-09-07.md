## What we learned

Confirmed by today's evidence, all of them already acted on:

- **A logged in Gateway can be useless.** Twice today the process was up, the
  port open and the login handshake fine, while no read ever answered. The
  watchdog needs a real bounded read, not a handshake, and now has one. The
  15:52 recovery proves the new check and the restart path work end to end.
- **A holiday has to be a closed day for every book, not only for the dead
  man's handle.** Book D ran a sweep and a pick on Labor Day; three books waited
  for an open that never came. Fixed and seen working from the 10:58 tick.
- **A socket timeout does not bound a call that keeps the connection alive.**
  A portfolio read ran 1,240 seconds with a 60 second timeout set, because the
  MCP server keeps the reply open while it waits on the Gateway. A wall clock
  deadline on the client is the fix, and it landed today.
- **The Mac's power state is part of the trading system.** Everything above
  traces back to a laptop on battery with the lid down. No code fixes that.

Not proven today, only suspected: that the 15 to 18 minute tick gaps until
13:36 were the Mac dozing, and that the Gateway losing its upstream link at
22:35 Pacific on Sunday was tied to the time zone flip at the same minute.
