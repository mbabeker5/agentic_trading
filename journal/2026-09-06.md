## Regime finding (hub, 2026-09-06)

FINRA retired the pattern day trader rule effective 2026-06-04 (Regulatory Notice 26-10), phase-in to 2027-10-20. Migrated accounts have no $25,000 floor and no four-in-five count; instead an intraday margin deficit must be cured within about three business days, and four failures in twelve months can mean a 90-day restriction. IBKR says new margin accounts are generally not subject to the old rule but may be during the transition. Mo's account U28440091 opened 2026-09-02, so its regime is unknown. Tuesday's pre-flight reads the account's day-trades-remaining values to tell (three numbers means old regime, unlimited or absent means new). The day-trade counter and the hard limit on the two long-hold books become conditional on the old regime; under the new regime every order is checked against creating an intraday margin deficit, which the 100% gross cap should make impossible.
