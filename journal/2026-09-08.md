## What we learned

Confirmed by today's files:

- **A loss of internet on the Mac looks, to the watchdog, exactly like a dead
  Gateway.** It restarted a Gateway that was fine, then correctly reported that
  the restart did not take. The tell was in the alerts themselves: the Slack
  channel failed on a DNS lookup at the same minutes. The watchdog should check
  whether the internet is reachable before it blames the Gateway, and say "no
  internet" when it is not. Added to the backlog as item 21.
- **The dead man's handle works end to end on its safe path.** It fired at
  13:00:06 on a 23.7 minute old heartbeat during market hours, read the account,
  found nothing held, and did not trade. Nothing to change; it has now been
  seen doing its job once.
- **The 240 second tick cap works.** It killed the hung 09:30 tick at 09:34:43
  and the 09:35 tick ran on time. Yesterday a hang like that cost an hour and
  48 minutes.
- **The pre-flight budget works.** Verdict written at 09:00:05, in seconds.
  Yesterday it wrote nothing at all.
- **The paper API is told it has no real time entitlement.** With the Gateway
  freshly logged in at 12:55 and no competing session error in sight, IBKR
  answered SPY quote requests with 10089 and 10168 all afternoon. The learning
  from 2026-09-02, that real time sharing to paper was enabled, is not what the
  Gateway sees today.
- **"Not checked" reconciliation wording works live.** At 12:35 the tick said
  "not checked: the broker could not be read", marked the orphan check as not
  run, and halted nobody. Yesterday's version would have said "everything
  matched" about an account it could not see.

Suspected, not proven: that the competing IBKR login actually ended at some
point in the middle of the day. The 10197 error stops appearing and a different
error starts after the 12:55 relogin, which is consistent with the other session
having logged out, but a relogin can also change which error IBKR chooses to
report. Only a check of active sessions in Client Portal, or a clean quote
tomorrow morning, settles it.
