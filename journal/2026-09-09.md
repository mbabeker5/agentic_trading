Written by the 16:30 learning job. All times are New York.

back until about 12:15. IB Gateway had just done its nightly 2 AM restart and
logged in fine, then lost IBKR six minutes later and could never get back. It
tried 864 times over eight and three quarter hours. The watchdog saw the shut

Two things did go right and are worth saying first. Once the Gateway was back,
the paper account got prices for the whole afternoon, which has not happened

## What broke or nearly broke

1. **This Mac had no network from about 02:06 until about 12:15, and that one
   fact caused everything else.** Four separate records agree.
   - IB Gateway ran its own 2 AM restart, configured as `AutoRestartTime=02:00
     AM` in
     `/Users/mtalib/workspace_repos/personal_repo/agentic_trading/config/ibc.ini`.
     It logged in successfully at 02:00:08. At 02:05:52 its window changed to
     "Connecting to server...". By 03:00:56 it read "Attempt 98: connection
     error, will retry in 16 seconds...".
   - The watchdog stopped that one and started a fresh Gateway at 03:00:57. The
     new copy failed on its very first login attempt, at 03:01:01, and kept
     trying. The next timestamped line, eight and three quarter hours later,
     reads "Attempt 864: Authenticating..." at 11:46:47. All of this is in
     `/Users/mtalib/workspace_repos/personal_repo/agentic_trading/output/ibc_logs/ibc-3.24.2_GATEWAY-10.45_Wednesday.txt`.
   - Twenty of today's fifty alerts could not reach Slack. Every one failed with
     "nodename nor servname provided, or not known", which is a machine that
     cannot look up a web address. They failed continuously from 03:00:56 to
     11:40:05 and worked again from 12:15:01.
   - The Mac's own name changed. The Gateway log records the operating system on
     each start. At midnight it was `MTalibs-MacBook-Pro.local`. On the two
     restarts after midday it was `Mac.localdomain`, which is the fallback macOS
     uses when it cannot get a name from the network.

   This is the second day running with a network outage on this Mac. Yesterday
   it was about 22 minutes at lunchtime. Today it was over ten hours and it
   covered the whole morning session.

2. **The watchdog watched a dead Gateway for eight hours and twenty minutes and
   did not try to restart it.** This is the most expensive thing in the file and
   it is a rule, not a bug. It restarted the Gateway at 00:00:54 and again at
   03:00:57. Then nothing until 12:20:49. In between it raised "gateway port is
   still failing" at 04:00, 05:00, 06:00, 07:00, 08:00, 09:25, 09:55, 10:30,
   11:05, 11:40 and 12:15, eleven times, and made no repair attempt at any of
   them. Its own marker reads "Failing since 2026-09-09T04:00:05". Out of 90
   watchdog runs today, only three were completely clean.

   The reason is `_may_restart` at line 529 of
   `/Users/mtalib/workspace_repos/personal_repo/agentic_trading/agent/watchdog.py`.
   A shut port only earns a restart if there is no Gateway process running at
   all. The comment explains why, and the reasoning is sound in itself: starting
   a second Gateway on top of a running one gives two logins fighting over the
   same session, and a shut port on a live process might just mean the thing is
   still starting up. So it "stays a decision for a person". The trouble is that
   there is no clock on that patience, and the person it was left to could not
   be reached, because Slack was down for the same reason the Gateway was.

   There is a second half to the trap. The one check allowed to stop and restart
   a wedged Gateway is `ib_answers`, and it could not help, because it has to
   connect through the port to notice anything is wrong. The only check with
   permission to fix this was structurally blind to it.

   What broke the deadlock was luck. At about 12:17 the stuck Gateway's dialog
   lost focus, and by 12:20:40 the process was gone. That made `gateway_process`
   fail for the first time, which is a new failure, which is allowed to restart.
   The watchdog started a fresh Gateway at 12:20:49 and it logged in at 12:21:57,
   verified four ways in 65 seconds. A second wobble at 12:35 was fixed the same
   way by 12:40:12. From then on the Gateway was solid.

## What we learned

**The competing IBKR login is gone, and the market data problem underneath it is
real.** This answers yesterday's biggest open question and settles it. There was
not one mention of error 10197, or of a competing session, anywhere in today's
files: zero in the alerts database, zero in the recorder's error log, zero
everywhere else. Yesterday the recorder's error log alone held 1,758 of them.
What came back instead, all afternoon, was error 10089 and error 10168,
"requested market data requires additional subscription for API, delayed market
data is available". Yesterday's suspicion was right. Something was masking a
second problem, the mask is off, and the second problem is that the paper
account is not entitled to real-time prices through the API. That is now a fact
rather than a theory, and it is Mo's to fix in Client Portal.

**The paper account can get delayed prices, and delayed prices are half useful.**
This is genuinely new. From 12:25 to 16:05 the recorder collected 810 price
snapshots and every single one carried a price, with sensible bids and asks a
cent apart on SPY. That has not happened on any previous day. But the volume
field on those same snapshots is garbage, and volume is exactly what a momentum
strategy needs in order to judge whether a move is real. The five minute
historical bars in the same recording do carry believable volume: SPY's 15:45
bar shows 572,657 shares. So the workaround already exists inside the file we
collect, which is worth knowing before anyone concludes the delayed feed is
useless.

**A watchdog that only repairs a fault the first time it sees it will watch that
fault forever.** The rule is one restart attempt per outage, and a restart is
only considered when a check fails that was passing a moment before. Once
something is "still failing" it is reported and never acted on. Today that
turned a fixable Gateway into an eight hour outage. It is worth being fair to
the design: the rule exists to stop the watchdog stacking Gateways on top of
each other, which is a real and worse failure. The gap is that there is no
bounded wait after which patience turns back into action.

**A check that measures a symptom rather than the thing itself will lie during an
outage.** The loop_tick check reads a state file that only a successful tick
writes, so a loop that is running perfectly and exiting early looks identical to
a loop that is dead. On the one morning it mattered, it told us the wrong thing
for three hours.

**Two network outages in two days is a pattern, not an accident.** Yesterday
about 22 minutes, today over ten hours. Whatever the cause, it is not in this
repository, and it is now easily the largest source of lost trading days.
Backlog item 21, teaching the watchdog to tell "no internet" from "dead
Gateway", would have made today legible even though it would not have made it
shorter.

**What today does not prove.** Nothing at all about the strategies. No book made
a decision, no model was called, no order was priced, and no model money was
spent. Two trading days in, the five books have produced zero comparable
decisions between them. That is worth sitting with rather than glossing over.
The strategy work is finished and waiting, and both days so far have been lost
to a broker connection and a home network. Everything on the proposal list below
is plumbing, and it will stay that way until a book gets through a whole morning
with prices in front of it.

## Proposals for Mo

1. **Find out why this Mac keeps losing its network, before anything else in
   this list.** Two outages in two days, 22 minutes yesterday and over ten hours
   today, and today's cost the entire morning session. Nothing in this project
   can be coded around a Mac with no network. Worth checking whether the Wi-Fi
   is dropping, whether the router reboots overnight, and whether a wired
   connection is possible. Cost: an hour of looking, possibly a cable. Risk of
   not doing it: every trading day is a coin flip and the code cannot help.

2. **Let the watchdog restart a Gateway that has been up but silent for more
   than fifteen minutes.** Today's rule refuses to touch a process that is
   running, which is right for the first few minutes and wrong after that. The
   safe version already exists in the code: `ib_answers` restarts with a stop
   first, which cannot leave two Gateways fighting each other. The change is to
   allow that same stop-then-start for a shut port once it has been shut for a
   bounded time. Cost: a small change to `_may_restart` in
   `/Users/mtalib/workspace_repos/personal_repo/agentic_trading/agent/watchdog.py`
   plus a test. Benefit, measured against today: eight hours and twenty minutes
   of the trading day back.

3. **Move the two sweeps out from behind the Gateway check.** The Congress sweep
   at 07:30 and the insider sweep at 16:30 read government websites and nothing
   else. Neither should be stopped by a broker that is down. Either give them
   their own scheduled job, or let the tick run its sweep phase before it checks
   port 4002. Cost: small. Benefit: book D would have had a shortlist today, and
   both books keep working through any future Gateway outage.

4. **Sort out the market data entitlement in Client Portal.** This is the same
   ask as yesterday and it is now much better evidenced, because the competing
   login that muddied yesterday's reading is definitely gone. IBKR told the
   Gateway "requires additional subscription for API" 807 times today. Two
   things to look at: whether the 4.50 dollar subscription includes API access,
   and whether market data is shared with the paper account. Cost: minutes,
   possibly the 10 dollar bundle already on the backlog as item 10. Risk of not
   doing it: the books can price entries off delayed quotes, which is
   survivable, but they cannot judge volume, which is not.

5. **Make the recorder trust the bar volume and stop trusting the snapshot
   volume.** The five minute bars already carry believable share counts. The
   snapshot volume field is nonsense on this feed and the 100 billion guard is
   about a thousand times too generous to catch it. Two changes: prefer the
   bar's volume, and drop the ceiling to something a real stock could actually
   reach, nearer 10 billion. Cost: small, in
   `/Users/mtalib/workspace_repos/personal_repo/agentic_trading/agent/replay/record_day.py`.
   Risk of not doing it: 22 fake volume readings are already in today's
   recording, and a replay built on that data would size positions off them.

6. **Make lost alerts come back.** Backlog item 22, written yesterday about four
   lost alerts, cost twenty today, including every warning about the outage.
   Write a failed send to a pending file and flush it on the next successful
   send. Cost: small. Risk of not doing it: the days that matter most are
   exactly the days Slack cannot be reached, so Mo systematically hears about
   the quiet days and not the loud ones.

7. **Fix what the loop_tick check measures.** It should read the tick log, which
   a Gateway-down tick still writes, or a heartbeat the tick writes before it
   checks the Gateway. Then the alert would have said "the loop is running and
   cannot reach the Gateway", which is the truth and points at the right thing.
   Cost: small. Risk of not doing it: the next outage produces the same three
   hours of misleading alerts.

8. **Look at the MCP server before Thursday's open.** Two things. It stopped
   surviving between ticks today, which is new, and today's last one, process
   56318, is still running an hour after it was told to stop. Neither broke
   anything today, but the loop now depends on starting a fresh MCP server every
   five minutes, and there is a stuck one holding the port right now. Cost:
   reading
   `/Users/mtalib/workspace_repos/personal_repo/agentic_trading/output/mcp_logs/mcp_ibkr.log`
   and deciding whether to stop process 56318 by hand.

9. **Decide what to do about the IBKR password sitting in plain text on disk.**
   It is in
   `/Users/mtalib/workspace_repos/personal_repo/agentic_trading/output/gatewaystart.filled.sh`,
   on the line beginning `TWSPASSWORD=`. Two things soften it: the file is
   readable only by you (`-rwx------`), and `output/` is excluded by the
   project's `.gitignore`, so it has never been committed. It is still a live
   password in a working folder, and it is rewritten every time the Gateway
   starts. This is a decision for you rather than something to change quietly.
