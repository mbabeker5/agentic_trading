## AcademicEvidence review (2026-09-06)
Zarattini, Barbon, Aziz 2024 (SSRN 4729284), 7000+ US stocks 2016 to 2023: plain 5-min ORB 3.2 percent a year, Sharpe 0.48; with relative-volume selection 41.6 percent a year, Sharpe 2.81, drawdown 12 percent. The selection filter is the strategy. Filters: price above 5 USD, 14-day avg volume above 1M, 14-day ATR above 0.50 USD, relative volume at or above 100 percent measured on the first 5 minutes vs same window prior 14 days, trade top 20 by relative volume. Stop 10 percent of 14-day ATR (about 0.3 percent of price), NO profit target, hold to 4:00 close (SSRN 4416622 grid: 5 percent ATR stop, no target best). Only the 5-minute range works (15-min 17.4, 30-min 2.3, 60-min 4.1). Data-provider dispersion: identical code across Polygon, Alpaca, IQFeed, IBKR gave final equity 226k to 726k; range-high/low stops were the culprit, ATR stops converged. Direction = sign of the first 5-minute candle not the overnight gap; roughly half the trades short; VIX does not predict short profitability. Exit: compute at 15:45 and use Market-on-Close, not 15:55 market. Costs at 10k orders under 5 bp. Base rate: Barber et al., 97 percent of day traders likely lose; Chague and Giovannetti, 0.13 percent of 98,378 cleared a modest profit over 300 sessions. Caveat: all ORB results trace to three authors, one sells trading education; no independent replication; no out-of-sample after 2023. WebSearch was unavailable to this agent so contradicting literature is under-searched.
